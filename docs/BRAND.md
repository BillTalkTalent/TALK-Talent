# TALK Brand Guide

Use this for **anything TALK-branded that Claude makes**: slide decks, documents,
one-pagers, emails, social graphics and web pages. Don't invent a new palette or
typeface. Bill has flagged off-brand output more than once.

Reference deck: *Hired, Then Haunted* (TALK Community Webinar, October 29, 2026).

## Logo

The wordmark is **TALK** in heavy block capitals. **"TA" is always coral**, and
**"LK" is white on dark backgrounds and navy on light ones.**

| File | Use on |
|---|---|
| `public/brand/talk-logo-on-dark.png` | Navy backgrounds (TA coral, LK white) |
| `public/brand/talk-logo-on-light.png` | Light backgrounds (TA coral, LK navy) |

- On the cover, put the logo large at the top left.
- On content slides, put the logo small at the top right.
- Don't recolor the logo, and don't retype "TALK" in a font as a stand-in for it.

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Dark background (covers, statement slides) | `#0F1F35` |
| Primary navy: headings on light, cards on dark, callout bars | `#1A3A5C` |
| Coral accent: eyebrows on light, numbers, "TA" | `#E8503A` |
| Light blue: eyebrows and date lines on dark | `#8FA6E8` |
| Body text on light | `#3F5166` |
| Muted text and footers | `#78859E` |
| Light background | `#F5F8FC` |
| Second light tone, strips and borders | `#E3E8F5` |
| Body text on dark | `#CADCFC` |
| Cards on light | `#FFFFFF` |
| Secondary accents, used sparingly (numbered markers, polls) | `#7C3AED` purple, `#3B82F6` blue, `#EA580C` orange |

## Typography

- **Calibri / Carlito** for everything. Carlito is the free, metric-compatible
  Google Fonts version, so use it on the web and in artifacts.
- Headings are bold navy (`#1A3A5C`) on light backgrounds and white on dark.
- Eyebrows are small, bold, uppercase and letter-spaced, in coral on light or
  light blue on dark. They sit above every slide title.
- Body text uses regular weight.

## Layout patterns

- **Cover:** navy `#0F1F35` background with large overlapping circles in `#1A3A5C`
  and `#3F5166` at the top right. The logo goes at the top left, then the eyebrow,
  a big white title and a `#CADCFC` subtitle. A light blue date line sits at the bottom left.
- **Content slides:** light `#F5F8FC` background, with an occasional navy slide
  for contrast. The eyebrow and title sit at the top left, and the logo at the top right.
- **Callouts:** a navy `#1A3A5C` bar with white italic text.
- **Numbered items:** filled circles in coral, purple and blue.
- **Footer:** a small italic line in `#78859E` at the bottom left
  ("Title · TALK Community Webinar"), with the page number at the bottom right.
